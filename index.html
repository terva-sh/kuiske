Some conversations are for work. Some are for your closest people. Some start with a shared curiosity.

Kuiske brings your Matrix accounts into one desktop window, with room to keep each world its own. A calm interface for staying in touch, on an open network.

A closer look

Familiar, with space to breathe.

01 / THE DESKTOP
01

Your worlds, together.

Multiple Matrix accounts in one window. Separate account storage and credentials keep each account’s data scoped to it.

02

Keep the conversation.

Encrypted chats, saved history, drafts, and image and file sharing. Device verification and recovery help you settle in.

03

Part of an open network.

Join rooms, explore spaces, and talk across Matrix homeservers. Use the account and community you already call home.

Small beginnings. Thoughtful foundations.

Taking shape,
one conversation at a time.

Kuiske is in early development. The first desktop client is implemented and tested on Linux and Apple Silicon macOS. Public downloads aren’t available yet.

Mobile is a future possibility. For now, we’re giving desktop the time and attention it deserves.
